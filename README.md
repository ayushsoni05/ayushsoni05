### **About Me & Creative Vision**

I am a **Creative Full-Stack Software Engineer & Interactive Experience Developer** specializing in the intersection of **clean architecture, 3D WebGL graphics, and fluid Framer / Webflow design systems**. I create immersive, high-converting digital products where performance meets visual fidelity.

- **Frontend & Creative Tech**: React.js, Next.js, **Framer Motion**, **Webflow**, **Three.js**, React Three Fiber, GSAP animations, and Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend & Cloud Architecture**: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose, RESTful APIs, WebSockets, and Razorpay/Stripe fintech workflows.
- **AI & Systems Engineering**: Real-time HTML5 Canvas Computer Vision, multi-modal AI analyzers, and automated notification engines.
- **Design Systems**: Crafting custom Webflow CMS architectures, component-driven Framer designs, and transforming Figma prototypes into pixel-perfect React code.
